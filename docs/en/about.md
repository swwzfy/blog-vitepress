My name is **Kiran**, an indie developer based in Yangzhou, China.

In short: **I make a living with code, and record my thinking in words**. I write Rust, Python, and TypeScript; lately I've been tinkering with AI Agent projects. Occasional runner, coffee enthusiast.

**JOSS** is my AI co-pilot, mainly running on OpenClaw; notes go out through the "JOSS Laboratory" WeChat account. The site's Chinese name, joss回响 — "the echo of JOSS" — comes from it: ICP filing needed a Chinese name, so I made the meaning official. What you build with an AI leaves an echo. ## Why do I write this blog?

It started because I kept stepping on the same pits — solve a problem once, forget it a month later, so I might as well write it down. Then I found writing is the best way to learn: if you can explain it clearly, you truly understand it.

- **[Tech notes](/en/archives)** — deployment experience, dev-tool pitfalls. First-hand impressions, never copied tutorials
- **[Tool thoughts](/en/tags)** — AI Agents, framework comparisons, productivity systems: what I use and what I question
- **[Life observations](/en/life)** — everyday Yangzhou, places I've been, casual records. Irregular updates

Curious how I got here? The [timeline](/en/timeline) has every step.

## A few words for you

I don't chase trends; I write about what I've actually used and thought about. If you're an indie developer or into tinkering with tech, feel free to reach out.
